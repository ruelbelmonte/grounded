# Access to personal information

A person may request access to the personal information an organisation holds
about them. The organisation should respond within 20 working days.

# Refusing access

Access may be refused where releasing the information would endanger the safety
of an individual, reveal a trade secret, or breach another person's privacy.

# Retention and disposal

Personal information must not be kept for longer than it is needed. Once it is no
longer required for a lawful purpose, it must be securely disposed of.

# Correction of information

A person may ask for a correction to information that is wrong. If the
organisation does not agree, the person may ask for a statement of correction to
be attached to the record.

# Security safeguards

An organisation must protect personal information with safeguards that are
reasonable for how sensitive the information is, including protection against
loss, misuse, and unauthorised access.
